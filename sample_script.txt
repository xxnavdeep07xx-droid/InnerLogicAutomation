Every person you know is lying to you, and the scariest part is, most of them don't even realize it. Psychologists call it the illusion of transparency: the unshakeable belief that our inner thoughts are visible to the world. They're not. Your boss can't read your nervousness. Your friends can't sense your envy. You are a closed book, and so is everyone around you, which means every assumption you make about other people's motives is really a mirror of your own mind. The Stoics understood this two thousand years ago. They never judged intentions, only actions, because actions are all we can actually see. So today, catch yourself once, the moment you claim to know what someone else is thinking. You'll realize you were never reading their mind. You were only projecting yours.
