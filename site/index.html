How the upload buttons work (30 sec)
  1. Download the MP4 (or drag it straight from the player).
  2. YouTube — click Upload to YouTube: your clipboard now holds the full title + description, and YouTube Studio's upload page opens. Drag the file in, click the YT Auto-Fill bookmark (install below), press Next → Publish.
  3. Instagram — click Upload to Instagram: caption copied, instagram.com opens. Click ➕ → Reel, drag the file, click the IG Auto-Fill bookmark, hit Share.

No site can pick a file for you — that's browser security. Everything else is one click.
